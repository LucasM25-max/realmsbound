It keeps your preferred slower levelling, economy, and Prestige curve (`Stage 10–500` math and `Stages 501–1,000` Faerûn outline), weaves in **Elminster’s canonical disappearance** and **Regional Bastions**, and stretches **the Tutorial + Hero Levels 1–5 across Stages 1–25**—keeping your already-built Stage 1 intact save for minor tuning tweaks.

# Part 1: Rebalanced Progression, Economy & Freeplay Systems

## 1.1 Fixing Stage 1 & Re-Scaling Training Costs
Since Stage 1 yields ~`55 cp` total (including Snikkit at `9/10`), lowering starting prices fixes Beat 6 and feeds cleanly into a 1,000-stage curve:
* **Weapon Drills Rank 1:** **`10 cp`** (affordable after 3–4 Giant Rats; Rank 2 = `12 cp`, Rank 3 = `14 cp`, Rank 4 = `16 cp`, `×1.13` growth per rank).
* **Armour Drills Rank 1:** `15 cp` (`×1.13` growth).
* **Scavenging Rank 1:** `20 cp` (auto-collect at Rank 5 costs ~`130 cp` total—unlocked cleanly mid-tutorial).
* **Footwork Rank 1:** `25 cp` (`×1.15` growth).
* **Conditioning Rank 1:** `50 cp` (unlocks alongside Hero HP & Potions).
* **Tactics Rank 1:** `1 sp (100 cp)` (unlocks around the first Boss).
* **Milestone `×2` Jumps:** Ranks `10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 500…` (wider spacing past Rank 100 slows power spikes).

## 1.2 Why Progression Slows Down (Stages 1–1,000 Math)
To make Stages 1–1,000 last months of idle play while preserving **2024 PHB Hero Level XP thresholds** (`6,500 XP` at Lv 5 → `120,000` at Lv 13 → `355,000` at Lv 20) and bounded D&D accuracy:

1. **Slower Monster HP & Gold Scaling:**
   * **Stages 1–25 (Tutorial):** Tabletop-grounded HP (`7 → ~350 HP`) with gentle `×1.12` per-stage scaling so early gear and Training ranks shine.
   * **Stages 26–100:** Monster HP scales **`×1.10`** per stage (Bosses = `5×` stage mob HP; Stage 100 Boss ≈ `1.5M HP`). Gold scales **`×1.07`** per stage.
   * **Stages 101–500:** Monster HP scales **`×1.075`** per stage (Stage 500 Boss ≈ `10¹⁸–10¹⁹ HP`, `Q / aa` notation). Gold scales **`×1.055`** per stage.
   * **Stages 501–1,000:** Monster HP scales **`×1.06`** per stage (Stage 1,000 Boss ≈ `10³¹ HP`). Gold scales **`×1.045`** per stage.
   * **Why Walls Happen:** Gold grows slower than Training Rank costs (`×1.13`), and HP grows faster than Gold—forcing reliance on Forge levels, Dice Ladder steps, Gambits, Bastion perks, and Prestige resets.
2. **Expanding Kill Quotas:** Stages 1–25 (`10–20` kills) → Stages 26–100 (`20–25` kills) → Stages 101–300 (`25–30` kills) → Stages 301–500 (`30–35` kills) → Stages 501–1,000 (`40–50` kills).
3. **The "Adventurer's Pace" XP Rule:**
   * **Stages 1–25 ("Sage's Tutelage"):** Curated tutorial XP stretches **Hero Levels 1 → 5 (`0 → 6,500 XP`)** cleanly across the 25 tutorial stages.
   * **Stages 26+ (Freeplay):** Regular Frontier kills award **`round(3 × √CR + 2)` XP**, **First-Time Boss Kills** award **`50 × CR` Story XP** (Region Finales award `100 × CR`), plus small Stage First-Clear bonuses.
   * **Prestige Re-run Rule ("Familiar Foes"):** Stages already cleared in a previous run grant **10% XP**, preventing players from farming Level 20 inside Shadowdale.
4. **2024 Lair Bosses & Legendaries:** From Stage 26 onward, every 25-Stage Zone Boss and 100-Stage Region Finale uses 2024 *Monster Manual* Lair rules: **Legendary Actions** (interrupting your Round Clock with a tail sweep or lair pulse) and **Legendary Resistance** (blocking `1–2` Topple/Push/condition procs until stripped by hits or Command Strikes).

## 1.3 Narrative & Hub Evolution: Vanished Sage, Regional Guides & Bastions
* **Elminster’s Disappearance (Stage 25 → 26):** Right after you finish the Stage 25 tutorial finale and uncover the **Zhentarim coin**, Elminster vanishes from Shadowdale—mirroring *Adventures in Faerûn*'s **1501 DR** lore where his windmill tower stands locked with the sign: *"No trespassing. Violators should notify next of kin. Have a pleasant day."* He communicates only via **Offline Progress letters** and rare *Sending* quips, putting *your* hero at the center of the story.
* **Regional Guides:** Each 100-stage Act swaps in an iconic narrator from the books:
  * **Act 1 (Stages 26–100, Dalelands):** *Alias* & *Dragonbait*
  * **Act 2 (Stages 101–200, Baldur's Gate):** *Karlach Cliffgate*
  * **Act 3 (Stages 201–300, Moonshae Isles):** *Princess Caitlyn Kendrick*
  * **Act 4 (Stages 301–400, Icewind Dale):** *Sheriff Markham Southwell*
  * **Act 5 (Stages 401–500, Calimshan):** *Deryan Kaya*
* **Regional Bastions & Faction Renown (2024 DMG / FRAiF / FRHoF):**
  * Each Region unlocks a persistent **Bastion** (upgraded with gold sinks and hirelings for passive Materials, Dust, Gold, and Combat Buffs), starting with **Freedale** in the Dalelands.
  * Building **Faction Renown (`3 → 50`)** across the 8 Faerûn factions (*Harpers, Zhentarim, Emerald Enclave, Lords' Alliance, Order of the Gauntlet, Purple Dragon Knights, Red Wizards, Cult of the Dragon*) unlocks **Special Facilities** (e.g., *Harper Hideout, Zhentarim Travel Station, Thayan Tattoo Parlor*), **Harper Personas** (Skill Event disguises), and **Aurora’s Whole Realms Catalog** survival gear.

## 2.3 Detailed Script: Stages 2–25

### Act 0.1: Shadowdale's Farms & The River Ashaba (Stages 2–5)

#### **Stage 2 — *"Hedrun's Turnip Fields"***
* **Backdrop:** Mid-morning rolling turnip rows, wooden farmhouse, overturned cart spilling turnips, **Farmer Hedrun** waving a pitchfork from the roof, crows scattering.
* **Monsters (`12 kills`, `+100 XP` → `220 XP` cum.):** **Goblin Minion ×7** (carrying stolen turnips; drop `1 cp` joke "Turnip" junk), **Giant Fire Beetle ×5** (CR 0, ~9 HP, glowing orange abdomens lighting the soil; drop first Material: *Fire Beetle Gland*).
* **Beats & Unlocks:**
  1. **Armour Drills & Scavenging Unlock:** Elminster opens two more Training tracks (`15 cp` / `20 cp`). *"Hit harder, bleed less, pocket faster."*
  2. **Scavenging Rank 5 (Coin Magnet):** Once you hit Rank 5 (~`130 cp` total across Stages 1–2), coins fly to your purse automatically. *"There. No more grovelling for coppers."*
  3. **Optional Full Kit/Background Setup:** Unlocks quietly in the Hero tab (*Soldier/Criminal/Acolyte/Sage*, *Vanguard/Skirmisher/Purse*, *Fighting Style*—with `"Elminster, choose for me"` defaulted).

#### **Stage 3 — *"Hedrun's Cider Barn"*** *(First Level-Up Milestone!)*
* **Backdrop:** Noon at Hedrun's sun-dappled apple orchard and wooden barn; cider barrels leak sticky puddles, bees buzz around fallen apples.
* **Monsters (`12 kills`, `+110 XP` → `330 XP` cum.):** **Giant Rat ×6**, **Goblin Minion ×4**, **Giant Fire Beetle ×2**.
* **Beats & Unlocks:**
  1. **HERO LEVEL 2 (`300 XP`)!** Golden pillar of light, victory pose, New Power card flips: **ACTION SURGE** (*"Once per rest, take an entire extra turn"*), plus **Tactical Mind** passive unlocked.
  2. **Action Surge Guided Trigger:** A 4-goblin cluster rushes out of the barn. Time slows gently: *"A crowd! Press that glowing Action Surge button!"* Hero blurs into two immediate swings.
  3. **Short Rest Explained:** *"Recharges after a boss, or every minute and a half of scrapping."*
  4. **Stage Clear Gift:** Hedrun climbs down from his barn roof and hands you **3 Potions of Healing** (*"Bless you, stranger! Drink these if something bites back."*).

#### **Stage 4 — *"The Ashaba Ford"***
* **Backdrop:** Late morning at shallow stepping stones across the **River Ashaba**; shimmering animated water, reeds, dragonflies, old mill wheel turning on the far bank. Water splashes underfoot.
* **Monsters (`15 kills`, `+115 XP` → `445 XP` cum.):** **Stirge ×8** (sine-wave flight; *Blood Drain* latches onto Hero and ticks damage each Round until killed), **Goblin Minion ×4**, **Giant Fire Beetle ×3**.
* **Beats & Unlocks:**
  1. **Hero HP & Focus Targeting:** First Stirge latches on; HP bar flashes red. *"Flying noses with a thirst! Tap the Stirge to swat it first!"*
  2. **Second Wind & Conditioning:** Scripted dip below `50% HP` (protected by *Mystra's Patience* HP floor at 1 during Stages 1–4) → **Second Wind** glows (`2 charges/rest`). **Conditioning** track (`50 cp`) unlocks.
  3. **Potion Quick Slot:** Drink 1 of Hedrun's Potions (`"And when stubbornness fails, drink something red."`).

#### **Stage 5 — BOSS: *"The Ashaba Mill Dam"***
* **Backdrop:** Afternoon shadows at an old timber dam choked with goblin debris, stolen flour sacks, and squeaking waterwheels. Music adds brisk martial snare.
* **Pre-Boss Wave (`6 kills`):** **Goblin Minion ×4**, **Stirge ×2**.
* **Boss: BIG-EAR BAZZ, Forager-Captain** (Goblin Boss scout variant, ~85 HP, AC 13, **45-second Boss Timer**).
  * **Intro Card:** *"BIG-EAR BAZZ — Scourge of the Turnip Wagon."*
  * **Mechanic:** At `50% HP`, blows a cracked horn to summon **2 Goblin Minions**; swings a stolen butcher's cleaver. (Easy first boss to teach Boss Timers and opening with Action Surge!)
* **Rewards (`+135 XP` → `580 XP` cum.):**
  * **Boss Chest (Uncommon Green glow):** Drops **Common/Uncommon Leather Gloves (+Damage)**, `1 gp 5 sp`, and **Materials Pouch** opens officially.
  * **Unlocks:** **Buy ×10** toggle in Training (`"One rank at a time? How quaint."`) + **Tactics Training Track** (`1 sp`).

### Act 0.2: Old Dale Road & Skrag's Bridge (Stages 6–10)

#### **Stage 6 — *"The Burned Waystation"***
